# Shopping List

This context covers how one household prepares shopping lists and uses them while shopping along familiar store routes.

## Language

**Household**:
The group of people who share Shopping Lists and Store Routes.

**Shopping List**:
A named household collection of intended purchases. It is independent of any Store.
_Avoid_: Checkbox list

**Product**:
A household-specific identity for something the household can purchase repeatedly.
_Avoid_: Catalog item, shopping item

**List Entry**:
The occurrence of a Product on a Shopping List, including details and shopping progress specific to that occurrence.
_Avoid_: Row, item

**Store**:
A named physical place where the household shops and that can have a Store Route.
_Avoid_: Supermarket, retailer, chain, brand

**Store Route**:
A saved ordering of Route Sections that reflects the household's usual path through a Store.
_Avoid_: Supermarket route, checklist order

**Route Section**:
A named area encountered at one position along a Store Route.
_Avoid_: Coordinate, map region
